What data we collect

Only what you enter yourself when placing an order: your name, phone number or email address, and a comment on the order. We do not collect or store payment details: payment happens outside the website.

Together with the order we store where you came from: the address of the site that referred you and the page you arrived at. We need it to understand which pages of the shop work. This record contains no name, no number and no device.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your order and to agree on delivery and payment. The data is not used for any other purpose, and it is not passed on or sold to third parties.

On what grounds

On the grounds of your consent. You give it by submitting the order form, and you can withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

Who else has access

The shop's hosting provider has technical access: it stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes.

How long we keep it

As long as it is needed to handle the order and to answer possible questions about it. An order that is fully settled and has no claims against it is deleted at your request.

Your rights

You can find out what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent to its processing. Write to {{.Shop.OwnerEmail}} and we will reply.
